If the power goes off

Report a power cut by calling 105. It is free and puts you through to your local network operator, SP Energy Networks in Flintshire. You can also follow cuts in your area on our power cuts page.

Join the Priority Services Register if someone in your home relies on power for medical equipment, has a disability, or needs extra help. Network operators use it to support people during a cut. It is free: thepsr.co.uk.

Have the basics ready: a torch with spare batteries, a charged power bank, a battery or wind-up radio, some cash in small notes, and water and food that keep for about three days.

Questions

Will the lights go out?

Very unlikely. When spare power gets tight, NESO's first steps are to ask power stations to be ready, pay large users to cut back, and buy power from abroad. Planned power cuts are a last resort and have not been used in Britain for decades. Power cuts in Flintshire are almost always down to faults on the local network, which you can follow on our power cuts page.

What is an Electricity Margin Notice?

A message from NESO to the power industry saying spare capacity looks thinner than it would like at a certain time, usually a cold, still evening. It asks generators to offer more power. It is a signal to the market, not a warning to households.

Why is so much of it gas?

When the wind drops, gas power stations such as Connah's Quay fill the gap because they can be turned up quickly. On windy days wind can supply more than half of Britain's electricity and gas falls back.

Why is there no solar on the chart?

The live national figures only include power stations and wind farms connected to the high-voltage grid. Solar panels on roofs and fields, and small wind farms, feed into local networks and are not measured live in the same way, so wind is a little higher than shown here and solar adds more on sunny days.

What does the frequency mean?

The grid runs at 50 hertz. When more power is being used than made, it dips slightly; when there is too much, it rises. NESO keeps it between 49.8 and 50.2 almost all of the time. A big drop below 49.5 is rare and can trigger automatic disconnections, as happened in August 2019.
