Flintshire gritting routes

The roads Flintshire Council grits first when ice or snow is forecast. These Priority 1 routes are the main roads, busy bus routes and links between towns and villages: around 560km, or 45% of the county's roads.

Priority 1 gritting route

Which roads get gritted?

Flintshire Council sorts every road in the county into three groups.

  • Priority 1 (on the map): the main road network, gritted as a precaution when ice or snow is forecast. All roads with eight or more buses an hour are in this group.
  • Priority 2: treated in snow and long spells of ice once Priority 1 roads are done, if the council has enough people and salt.
  • Priority 3: everything else. Treated only after Priority 1 and 2, and only during normal working hours.

If conditions get worse, crews go back to the Priority 1 roads first. Pavements and cycle paths are not part of the gritting routes.

Gritted does not mean ice-free

Salt takes time and traffic to work. Rain can wash it away, and when rain turns to snow at rush hour the gritters get stuck in the same traffic as everyone else. On a wet night that clears quickly, gritting can only start once the rain stops. Drive carefully even on a gritted road.

Trunk roads such as the A55 and A494 are looked after by the North and Mid Wales Trunk Road Agent for the Welsh Government, not the council. Check them on our roadworks and traffic page.

Grit bins, problems and requests

Grit bins are for public roads and pavements, not private drives. To report an empty or damaged grit bin, a problem on a gritting route, or to ask for a road to be added, use Flintshire Council's gritting pages. The council says every road is surveyed before winter to set its priority.

Before you set off, check the weather and warnings.
