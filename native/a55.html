Heading for Mold? You do not use any of these. Stay on the A55 west from Ewloe and leave at junction 33B, where the A494 splits off for Mold.

What happened to the "Red Route"?

The Welsh Government's preferred plan, chosen in 2017, was a new 13km dual carriageway from A55 junction 33 at Northop to the A494 and A550 north of Deeside Parkway, via the Flintshire Bridge. It became known locally as the Red Route.

It is not going ahead. In February 2023 the Welsh Government's response to its Roads Review said the Flintshire Corridor scheme had been replaced by a wider approach to transport in the area, and its project page now says the scheme is not going ahead.
