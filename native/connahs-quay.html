Connah's Quay is the largest town in Flintshire, with about 16,800 people, on the south bank of the Dee estuary between Shotton and Flint. It grew up as a working port shipping coal and building ships, and today it is home to Wepre Park, Coleg Cambria's Deeside campus and Connah's Quay Nomads.

A short history

The town is usually said to take its name from James Connah, who lived at the Quay House in the 18th century. Trade grew after the New Cut, a straightened channel for the River Dee completed in 1737, moved shipping away from Chester. The early 19th century dock basin on Dock Road, built for exporting coal, is now Grade II listed, and shipyards here built vessels such as the schooner Kathleen and May, launched in 1900.

Railways brought bricks and coal from Buckley down to the river, and the town had its own station from 1870 until 1966. Over the 20th century it absorbed the neighbouring hamlets of Golftyn, Kelsterton and Wepre. The Flintshire Bridge, which carries the A548 over the Dee, opened in 1998, next to the gas-fired Connah's Quay power station run by Uniper.

Questions

Is Wepre Park free?

The park is free to enter, but parking is not. Since 27 July 2026 the car parks cost £1 for up to two hours, £2 for up to four hours or £4 for the day, between 9am and 9pm, with a £50 annual permit available. See our Wepre Park guide.

Where do Connah's Quay Nomads play?

The men's team play their Cymru Premier home games at the Essity Stadium in Flint, under a ground share since the 2023/24 season. Nomads Women play at Deeside Stadium. The club won the Cymru Premier in 2019/20 and 2020/21 and first played in Europe in 2016.

Which A55 junction is for Connah's Quay?

Junction 33A, Northop Hall, is signed for Connah's Quay via the B5126. Our A55 and A494 junction guide covers every junction in Flintshire.

When are the bins collected?

Flintshire County Council collects black bins every three weeks, and recycling and food waste every week. Check your own collection days on the council's website.

Is Northern Gateway in Connah's Quay?

No. The Northern Gateway housing and employment development is on the former RAF Sealand and Corus land at Sealand and Garden City, across the river.
