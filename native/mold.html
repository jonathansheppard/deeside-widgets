Mold is the historic county town of Flintshire, a market town of about 10,000 people at the foot of the Clwydian Range. It is home to Theatr Clwyd, Wales's largest producing theatre, a twice-weekly street market, the Norman castle mound at Bailey Hill and the story of the Mold gold cape.

A short history

The town grew up around Bailey Hill, a Norman motte and bailey castle first recorded in 1146, which is now a public park holding a Green Flag Award. Mold's parish church, St Mary's, is Grade I listed: its nave dates from the late 15th and early 16th centuries.

In 1833 workmen digging a burial mound at Bryn yr Ellyllon, just outside the town, found the Mold gold cape, a Bronze Age ceremonial cape made around 1900 to 1600 BC. The original is in the British Museum, and Mold Museum on Earl Road has a replica. In 1869 the town saw the Mold Riot, when soldiers fired on a crowd after a court case linked to Leeswood Green Colliery and four people were killed.

The novelist Daniel Owen (1836 to 1895), author of Rhys Lewis, Enoc Huws and Gwen Tomos and regarded as the national novelist of Wales, was born in Mold. His statue stands in the town, which also hosts the Gŵyl Daniel Owen festival in October.

Questions

When is Mold market?

The street market runs every Wednesday and Saturday from 9am to 3.30pm, with more than 70 stalls along the High Street and Daniel Owen Square. The indoor market is open Monday to Saturday, and there is a farmers' market on the first and third Saturdays of the month at St Mary's Church Hall.

What is Theatr Clwyd?

Wales's largest producing theatre, on the hill above Mold. It opened in 1976, celebrates its 50th anniversary in 2026 and reopened in July 2025 after a £50 million redevelopment.

Where can I park in Mold?

Flintshire County Council runs pay and display car parks at Griffiths Square, Grosvenor Street, King Street, Love Lane, Meadow Place and New Street, plus the multi-storey by Theatr Clwyd.

Which A55 junction is for Mold?

Heading west, leave at junction 33B onto the A494 for Mold. You can also use junction 33 at Northop and the A5119. Our A55 and A494 junction guide has the details.

Where are the council offices now?

Flintshire County Council moved its main offices from County Hall in Mold to Tŷ Dewi Sant in Ewloe in early 2025.

When are the bins collected?

Black bins are collected every three weeks, and recycling and food waste every week. Check your own days on the council's website.
