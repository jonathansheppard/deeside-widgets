How we measure this

We count every passenger train timetabled to run between Wrexham General and Bidston, in both directions, each day. A train counts as cancelled if it did not run at all, and part-cancelled if it skipped some of its stops, for example by terminating early.

"Within 3 minutes" means the train ran its full route and was never more than three minutes behind the timetable at any station where its time was recorded.

The figures come from National Rail's Historical Service Performance data. It does not include some trains that were changed at short notice on the day, so the cancellation figures here are a minimum: the true number can be a little higher.
